Installation Guide

How to Install Y Posts

1

Mark the Fence Line

Run a string line between end posts. Mark Y post positions at 2–4 m intervals depending on fence type and terrain.

2

Drive the Post

Use a manual post driver or hydraulic driver. Drive vertically to a depth of 500–600 mm. The pointed tip penetrates hard ground easily.

3

Attach Wire / Mesh

Use the pre-punched nail holes (if provided) or clip wire through the Y-flange to secure field fence, wire or mesh at each line wire.

4

Check Alignment

After all posts are in, check alignment with the string line. Adjust any posts that have shifted during driving. Tension the wire evenly.

Pro Tip

On rocky or very hard ground, pre-drill a pilot hole with a steel rod slightly smaller than the post width. This prevents the Y post from bending at the tip during driving.
