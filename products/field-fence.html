Installation Guide

How to Install Field Fence

1

Set Corner & End Posts

Install heavy-duty end posts (≥100 mm diameter) at corners and terminations. Brace each end post with a diagonal stay and horizontal rail for tension resistance.

2

Drive Line Posts

Space intermediate Y posts or timber posts at 3–5 m intervals. Drive to a uniform depth (typically 600 mm) using a post driver.

3

Unroll & Tie Off

Unroll the field fence along the fence line starting from an end post. Tie the first line wire to the end post with hog rings or tie wire at every horizontal wire.

4

Tension & Secure

Use a wire strainer or come-along to tension the fence evenly. Attach to line posts with staples or clips. Avoid over-tensioning — the hinge joint needs room to flex.

Pro Tip

For uneven terrain, follow the ground contour rather than stretching the fence level. The hinge-joint design naturally absorbs undulation without losing tension.
