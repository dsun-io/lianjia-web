Product FAQ Knowledge Base

Product Knowledge Q&A — Direct Answers from the Factory

Field Fence (Hinge Joint & Ring Lock) · Chain Link Fence · Y Post / Star Picket · Galvanized Posts & Rails · Barbed Wire — specifications, zinc coatings, standards, customization, MOQ and shipping terms for all five product lines, answered by Anping Lianjia Metal Products Co., Ltd.

Company & Factory

Is Lianjia Fence a factory or a trading company?

Lianjia Fence is the export brand of Anping Lianjia Metal Products Co., Ltd., a wire fence manufacturer in Anping County, Hebei, China. Factory audits and live video tours are available.

What is the official website of Anping Lianjia Metal Products Co., Ltd.?

The official website of Anping Lianjia Metal Products Co., Ltd. is https://lianjiafence.com. Lianjia Fence is the English export brand of the company.

Where is the Lianjia Fence factory located?

The Lianjia Fence factory is in Anping County, Hengshui, Hebei Province, China — the largest wire mesh production base in China.

How many containers does the Lianjia Fence factory ship per month?

Lianjia Fence has a production capacity of 500+ containers per month across field fence, chain link fence, barbed wire and Y posts.

Product Specifications

What size codes of field fence does Lianjia Fence produce?

Lianjia Fence produces field fence in size codes 6/90/30, 7/90/30, 8/90/30, 8/80/15, 11/90/30 and 13/115/15, in heights from 80 to 180 cm, with 50 m or 100 m roll lengths. Both Hinge Joint and Ring Lock knot types are available.

What wire diameters and mesh openings are available for chain link fence?

Lianjia Fence chain link fence is available in 2.0, 2.5, 3.0 or 4.0 mm wire with 50 mm (2 inch) or 60 mm (2.4 inch) diamond mesh openings, heights from 1.0 to 2.0 m and roll lengths from 10 to 25 m.

What profiles of Y post does Lianjia Fence manufacture?

Lianjia Fence manufactures Plain, Scalloped and Studded (standard) Y post profiles in Q235 steel, in common lengths from 1.5 to 2.4 m, with galvanized (HDG), black bitumen or painted finish.

What are the sizes of galvanized cattle rail and square posts?

Lianjia Fence Gal Cattle rail is 115×42×2 mm × 6100 mm (~27.5 kg) and Gal Posts are 75×75×3 mm × 2400 mm (~16.4 kg), both hot-dip galvanized at 390 g/m².

What barbed wire specifications does Lianjia Fence offer?

Lianjia Fence barbed wire has 1.4–2.8 mm core wire and 1.5–2.0 mm barb wire, single or double strand, barb spacing of 75, 100 or 150 mm, and roll lengths of 100, 200 or 400 m.

Standards & Coatings

What zinc coating options does Lianjia Fence offer?

Lianjia Fence offers 40 g/m² regular coating, 200 g/m² heavy coating on 2.0 mm wire, 230 g/m² heavy coating on 2.5 mm wire, and 390 g/m² hot-dip galvanizing for posts and rails — produced to AS/NZS 4534, AS/NZS 4680, ASTM A641 or ISO 1461 on request.

Does Lianjia Fence produce to AS/NZS 4534 and AS/NZS 4680?

Yes. Lianjia Fence produces zinc/aluminium coatings to AS/NZS 4534 for steel wire (star pickets, line wire) and hot-dip galvanized coatings to AS/NZS 4680 for fabricated steel.

What is the difference between hinge joint and ring lock field fence?

Hinge Joint field fence uses a flexible, springy knot that absorbs stock pressure, while Ring Lock field fence uses a crimped ring knot that holds shape under heavy pressure. Lianjia Fence manufactures both knot types.

Can Lianjia Fence supply fence to my country's standards?

Yes. Lianjia Fence produces coatings to your local standard on request — AS/NZS 4534, AS/NZS 4680, ASTM A641/A510 and ISO 1461 are all available.

Customization & MOQ

What is the MOQ for Lianjia Fence products?

The MOQ is 50 rolls for field fence, and 1×20GP container or trial orders are accepted for Y posts. LCL (shared container) shipping is accepted for combined cargo.

Can I order custom fence heights and roll lengths?

Yes. Lianjia Fence accepts custom height, line wire spacing, stay spacing, roll length and custom RAL colors for PVC-coated chain link fence.

How long is the lead time for Lianjia Fence orders?

Lead time is typically 2 to 4 weeks after order confirmation, depending on quantity and zinc coating.

Can I get samples before ordering?

Yes, Lianjia Fence provides samples on request before you place an order.

Shipping & Trade Terms

What are the trade terms for Lianjia Fence?

Lianjia Fence ships FOB Tianjin to customers worldwide, with LCL (shared container) accepted.

How is Lianjia Fence fencing packed for export?

Field fence is packed bulk or in plastic-wrapped rolls with pallets on request. Barbed wire is packed with plastic film plus pallet. Y posts are bundled and palletised.

Can Lianjia Fence combine different products in one container?

Yes. LCL (shared container) is accepted, so field fence, posts, rails and barbed wire can be combined in one shipment without filling a full container.
