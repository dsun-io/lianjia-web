About Us

Factory-Direct Wire Fence from China's Wire Mesh Capital

Anping Lianjia Metal Products Co., Ltd. is a wire fence manufacturer based in Anping, Hebei — the largest wire mesh production base in China. With 20 years of production and a capacity of 500 containers per month, we specialize in field fence, chain link fence, barbed wire and Y posts, supplying wholesalers, fencing contractors and importers worldwide. As a factory we control quality from wire drawing to hot-dip galvanizing to export packing. Zinc coatings are supplied to AS/NZS 4534, AS/NZS 4680, ASTM A641 and other local standards on request, and every order is checked for wire diameter, coating weight, mesh size and tensile strength before shipment. Factory audits and live production videos are welcome at any time.
